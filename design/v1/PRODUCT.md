## Platform

adaptive

## Users and purpose

Music listeners inspect local files; DJs monitor the combined output of a selected device. Beatglass makes tempo, key and chord evidence accessible without interrupting playback.

## Capabilities and constraints

Native desktop on Linux, macOS and Windows. Source files are preserved. Estimates may be provisional, ambiguous or unavailable; mixed output is not isolated decks. Selection changes and cancellation clear stale evidence. Cache reuse requires verified source and dependency identity. Native implementation is in progress.

## Principles

- Attribute evidence to its source and communicate uncertainty.
- Make interruptions and recovery visible.
- Keep the overlay readable and safely unlockable.
